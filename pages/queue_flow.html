SMHC Counter & Department Queue Flow

1. Ticket Creation
Generate Ticket
Patient registers at kiosk or reception. Entry saved in daily_queue.
daily_queue ticketing
Waiting Queue Monitor
Counter attendants view available tickets that are not yet assigned to any counter or department.
queue_board.php waiting-list
2. Counter Interaction
Call from Counter
Attendant calls selected ticket. Entry created in queue_calls with status ACTIVE.
queue_calls Call Selected
Serve / Provide Service
Ticket appears in the “Currently Serving” panel and the public display. Attendant may complete or reroute.
announce customer_board
Route to Department
For additional services, the ticket is pushed to department_queue with status WAITING.
department_queue Route
3. Department Workflow
Department Calls Ticket
Department user moves entry to CALLED. Public board announces via “Now Serving”.
department_call public display
Department Completes
When finished, status becomes DONE. Optionally auto-routes back to another department or counter.
department_complete optional re-route
Counter Follow-up (Optional)
If routed back, the counter sees it in “Routed From Departments”, can call again or complete the journey.
counter_queue Complete Routed
4. Completion
Mark as Done
Both counter and department can finalize their steps. Ticket history remains for reporting.
DONE reporting
Return to Queue (Exception)
Counters may return tickets to the waiting pool if a customer is not present.
RETURNED retry
Legend
• Blue tables hold primary counter data.
